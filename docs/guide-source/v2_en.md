@@CH 1
@@TITLE Getting Started with BizTrack

## 1.1  What is BizTrack?

BizTrack is a digital system for managing your business — sales, purchases, stock, customers, suppliers, staff and finances — for one store or several stores at once. It runs in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone, and it can also be installed on the device like an app (section 1.9). Everything is available in English and Swahili.

This is Version 2 of BizTrack. The screens keep the names you already know, but they open faster, lists turn into easy-to-read cards on a phone, reports have totals and charts, and receipts can be printed straight to the printer.

## 1.2  Logging In

Every user has their own account with an email address and a password.

1. Open BizTrack in your browser (or the installed BizTrack app).
2. Enter the email address you registered with.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click [[Login]].

> NOTE: If your store administrator gave you an account, use the email and password they provided. You can change the password later under [[My Profile]] → [[Change Password]].
> WARNING: To protect accounts, sign-in pauses for a few minutes after 10 wrong passwords for the same email within 15 minutes. The message tells you how long to wait. Signing in correctly never counts against you, and other people are not affected. If you have forgotten the password, use [[Forgot password?]] instead of guessing.

## 1.3  Registering a New Account

1. On the login page, click [[Register here.]]
2. Fill in the [[Store Code]] of the store you are joining, your [[Full Name]], your [[Email]] and a [[Password]].
3. Click [[Register]].
4. Open the email you receive and verify your address (also check your spam folder).
5. Your account starts disabled and without a role. The store owner or manager gives you a role and enables the account (section 9.1). After that, log in with your email and password.

> NOTE: The Store Code identifies the store. Your store administrator can give it to you — it is listed under [[Stores]]. The administrator is told when someone has registered and is waiting for access.

## 1.4  Forgot Password

1. On the login page, click [[Forgot password?]]
2. Enter the email address registered to your account.
3. Open the email you receive and click the reset link.
4. Enter a new password and confirm it. A new password must be at least 6 characters.

> NOTE: When you change your password later from [[My Profile]] → [[Change Password]], the new password must be at least 8 characters long.
> TIP: You may ask for a reset email about 10 times an hour for the same address. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder before asking again.

## 1.5  The Dashboard — Your Business at a Glance

After you log in you land on the Dashboard, with a greeting and the name of the store you are working in. Use [[Refresh]] to reload the figures. It shows:

- [[Today's Sales]] — revenue and number of sales today, with the change compared with yesterday.
- [[This Month]] — revenue and number of sales for the month so far.
- [[Amount Due]] — money still owed on today's sales. Click it to open Debits.
- [[Expenses]] — what you spent this month.
- [[Revenue Trend]] — a chart of revenue over the recent days.
- [[Payment Method Breakdown]] — how customers paid.
- [[Recent sales]] and [[Top Products]], with [[View all]] to open the full list.
- [[Quick Actions]] — shortcuts to the pages you use most, such as Invoice, Day Report, Products, Customers, Purchase Orders, Expenses and Sales Reports. Only the shortcuts your role may use are shown.

If your role has BizTrack AI, short AI cards with a summary of your business also appear (see Chapter 13).

> NOTE: If your role does not include the dashboard figures or the sales reports, the Dashboard shows a short notice, today's date and a card for each page your permissions allow — for example Invoice, Sales History, Customers, Products, Current Stock, Purchase Orders, Expenses and Sales Reports. Click a card to open that page. If no card is shown, ask your store administrator to give your role the permissions you need (Chapter 9).

## 1.6  Understanding the Main Menu

The main menu is on the left of the screen. It takes you to every part of the system: [[Dashboard]], [[Sales]], [[Purchases]], [[Inventory]], [[Categories]], [[Reports]], [[Expenses]], [[Users]], [[Stores]], [[Messaging]], [[Settings]], [[Subscription]], [[BizTrack AI]] and [[User Guide]]. You only see the entries your role is allowed to use.

- On a computer: click the arrow above the menu to collapse or expand the menu labels.
- On a phone or tablet: tap the menu icon (≡) at the top left to open the menu as a slide-out drawer.
- Click a section such as [[Sales]] to open its list of pages (for example [[Invoice]], [[Pending Orders]], [[Sales History]]).
- At the top of every page you will find the bell for notifications, the help icon ([[Help & support]]), the button to lock the screen, and your profile menu.

## 1.7  Selecting a Store

If your business has more than one store, use the store selector at the top of the page. All the data you see — products, sales, reports — belongs to the store you have selected.

1. Click the name of the current store.
2. Choose a different store from the list.
3. BizTrack loads the data of the store you chose.

> WARNING: Make sure you are in the correct store before recording sales, purchases or stock adjustments — each store keeps its own stock and records.

## 1.8  Session Lock (Inactivity)

To protect your business data, BizTrack locks your session when you have been inactive for a while. The time limit is set by your administrator under [[Settings]] → [[Security]]. You are not logged out — the screen asks for your password and you continue where you stopped. You can also lock the screen yourself with the lock button at the top of the page.

1. Enter your password on the lock screen.
2. Click [[Unlock]] to return to the page you were working on.

> NOTE: If you prefer to leave completely, click [[Sign out instead]] on the lock screen, or [[Sign out]] in your profile menu.

## 1.9  Installing BizTrack as an App

BizTrack can be installed so that it opens in its own window, with its own icon, like any other app — on a computer, a phone or a tablet.

1. Open BizTrack in your browser and sign in.
2. If your browser offers it, BizTrack shows an install message. Accept it. You can also use the browser's own menu (for example "Install app" or "Add to Home screen").
3. Open BizTrack from the new icon.

When a new version of BizTrack is released, the app shows a message offering to update. Accept it to continue with the new version.

> NOTE: Installing is optional. BizTrack works exactly the same in the browser.

@@CH 2
@@TITLE Products & Categories

Before you start selling, add your products to the system. This is done under [[Inventory]] → [[Products]], with [[Categories]] to keep them organised.

## 2.1  Adding a New Product

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Products]] › [[New Product]]
PERM: view_products / create_products

1. Go to [[Inventory]] → [[Products]].
2. Click [[New Product]].
3. Fill in the [[Product Name]].
4. Select the [[Unit]] (for example Piece, Kilogram, Box, Crate, Litre).
5. Select the [[Product Category]] (if it does not exist yet, create it first under [[Categories]] — see section 2.3).
6. Enter the [[Barcode]] — type it, or use the scan button to read the product's real barcode with the camera.
7. Enter the [[Cost Price]] and the [[Selling Price]]. The selling price applies to the default price category (section 2.4).
8. Enter the [[Current Stock]] on hand (the opening stock).
9. Enter the [[Alert Quantity]]: BizTrack warns you when stock falls to or below this number.
10. Choose the [[Status]]: [[Active]] (can be sold) or [[Inactive]] (hidden from selling).
11. If expiry tracking is enabled in Settings, fill in the [[Manufactured Date]] and [[Expiry Date]].
12. Add a [[Product Description]] and an [[Image]] (both optional).
13. Click [[Save]].

> NOTE: The barcode must be unique — two products cannot share the same barcode. To set different prices for other customer groups, use Price Categories and the Price List (sections 2.4 and 2.5).

## 2.2  Editing, Printing a Barcode or Deleting a Product

1. Go to [[Inventory]] → [[Products]].
2. Search for the product by name or barcode.
3. Use the row actions: [[View]], [[Edit]] (to change its details), [[Print Barcode]] (to print a barcode label) or [[Delete]].
4. To remove several products at once, tick them in the list and use the delete button that appears.

> WARNING: Deleting a product cannot be undone. If a product has sales or purchase history, it is safer to edit it and set its [[Status]] to [[Inactive]] instead of deleting it.

## 2.3  Product Categories

PATH: [[Categories]] › [[Product Categories]]

Categories group your products (for example Beverages, Building Materials, Clothing) to make searching easier and to break reports down by category.

1. Go to [[Categories]] → [[Product Categories]].
2. Click [[New Category]].
3. Enter a [[Category Name]] and a [[Description]] (optional).
4. Click [[Create]].

## 2.4  Price Categories

PATH: [[Categories]] › [[Price Categories]]

If your store uses different prices for different customer groups (for example Retail and Wholesale), use Price Categories. This feature must first be enabled in Settings under Multi-Price Support.

1. Go to [[Categories]] → [[Price Categories]].
2. Click [[New price category]], enter a name (for example Wholesale) and create it.
3. Go to [[Inventory]] → [[Price List]] to set each product's price for that category.

## 2.5  Price List

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Price List]]

Here you view and update the prices of all products quickly, without opening each product.

1. Go to [[Inventory]] → [[Price List]].
2. If you use price categories, choose the [[Price Category]] you want to work on.
3. Search for the product you want to reprice.
4. Click [[Update price]] and enter the new selling price (and cost price where it is shown).
5. Click [[Update]].

> NOTE: You can also import or export prices in bulk with Excel or CSV — see section 3.6.

@@CH 3
@@TITLE Inventory & Stock

The Inventory section gives you full control over your stock — quantities on hand, their value, adjustments, transfers between stores and physical stock counts.

## 3.1  Viewing Current Stock

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Current Stock]]

A list of all products and the quantity on hand. The [[Status]] column shows whether a product is well stocked, running low or out of stock, and [[Expiring soon]] marks batches close to their expiry date.

- In Stock — stock is above the alert quantity.
- Low Stock — stock has fallen to or below the alert quantity set on the product.
- Out of Stock — stock is zero or below.

Open a product with [[View]] to see its batches, with the batch number, cost price, selling price and dates. Use [[Edit]] to correct a batch, or [[Stock Adjustment]] to add or remove stock.

## 3.2  Stock Value

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Current Stock Value]]

Shows the total value of your stock at cost price — how much capital is tied up in your stock at any time.

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Stock Value History]]

Stock Value History records the value of your stock day by day, so you can see how it has grown or shrunk over time.

## 3.3  Stock Adjustment

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Stock Adjustment]]
PERM: view_stock_adjustment

Use this to correct quantities whenever there is a difference that is not the result of normal sales — for example damaged goods, theft, loss or stock found during a count.

1. Go to [[Inventory]] → [[Stock Adjustment]].
2. Click [[New Adjustment]].
3. Select the product (and the batch, if you use batch-wise tracking).
4. Choose [[Add stock]] or [[Remove stock]].
5. Enter the [[Quantity]].
6. Select a [[Reason]]: for example Expired, Customer Return, Stock Correction, Damaged, Found, Lost or Other.
7. Add [[Notes]] if needed.
8. Click [[Save]].

> WARNING: Stock cannot go below zero unless Allow Negative Stock is enabled in Settings.

Open any product with [[View]] to read its [[Adjustment History]]. You can filter the list by product, reason and dates.

## 3.4  Stock Count

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Stock Count]]

Use this when you count the stock on the shelves, to check that what the system shows matches what is really there.

1. Go to [[Inventory]] → [[Stock Count]].
2. Search for products or choose a category.
3. For each product, type the quantity you counted in [[Counted Qty]]. BizTrack shows the [[Difference]] against [[System Stock]] as you type.
4. Click [[Save Stock Count]] and confirm. BizTrack adjusts the stock to match your count and keeps the count in the list ([[Items checked]], [[Adjustments made]]).

> NOTE: Count regularly (for example monthly) to catch shrinkage early. Open a count with [[View]] to see exactly what was changed.

## 3.5  Stock Transfer Between Stores

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Stock Transfer]]

Available only if you have more than one store and the transfer feature is enabled in Settings (Allow Inter-Store Transfer). If it is off, the page says [[Inter-Store Transfer Disabled]].

1. Go to [[Inventory]] → [[Stock Transfer]].
2. Click [[New Transfer]].
3. Choose the [[Destination Store]] (the source is the store you are working in).
4. Add the products and the quantity to send; click [[Add Item]] for more.
5. Add [[Notes]] if needed and click [[Save]].
6. A user with the right permission approves the transfer with [[Approve]].
7. The other store then clicks [[Receive]], enters what actually arrived and confirms. Stock is moved when it is received.

> NOTE: A transfer can be cancelled with [[Cancel Transfer]] before it is received, or deleted by an authorised user. The other store sees the transfer in the same list, with the direction shown.

## 3.6  Bulk Import / Export

PATH: [[Inventory]] › [[Import / Export]]

Instead of adding products one by one, you can upload a long list of products, prices or stock in an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file.

1. Go to [[Inventory]] → [[Import / Export]] (or [[Settings]] → [[Bulk Operations]]).
2. Click [[Download Template]] to get the correct file layout.
3. Fill in your data using that template.
4. Click [[Click to select file]] and choose the file you filled in.
5. BizTrack shows how many rows are valid and lists the rows with errors (use the show-errors link).
6. Click the import button to import the valid rows.

> NOTE: Importing updates products that already exist (matched by barcode) and creates the new ones. The barcode is what identifies an existing product. You can also download your inventory as Excel at any time with [[Export Data]].
@@CH 4
@@TITLE Sales (POS)

This is where you record sales to customers — creating invoices, taking payments, tracking debts and managing customers. It is under the [[Sales]] menu. On a phone, the items of a sale and the lists on these pages appear as cards that are easy to read and touch.

## 4.1  Creating a New Sales Invoice

PATH: [[Sales]] › [[Invoice]]
PERM: create_invoice

1. Go to [[Sales]] → [[Invoice]].
2. Add products to the sale: pick from the [[Select a product]] list (it searches all your products), type or scan the [[Barcode]], or tap [[Scan barcode]] on a phone to use the camera.
3. For each product added, change the [[Quantity]] and the [[Price]] if your store allows it. On a phone each product is a card with its own fields; on a computer the items are in a table.
4. If discounts are enabled, enter a [[Discount]] per item. If VAT is enabled, the [[Tax]] is calculated for you.
5. Choose a customer if you need one (for a credit sale or a registered customer) with [[Select Customer]]; for a walk-in customer type a name or leave it as [[Walk-in Customer]]. If price categories are enabled, choose the [[Price Category]].
6. Check the [[Subtotal]] and the [[Total]] in the summary (below the items on a phone).
7. Choose the [[Payment Method]] — [[Cash]], mobile money or bank transfer (for bank transfer, enter the [[Reference Number]]) — and enter the [[Amount Paid]]. [[Full payment]] fills in the whole amount. BizTrack shows the [[Change]] or the [[Amount Due]].
8. Add [[Notes]] if needed. If your store allows past dates, you can change the [[Sales Date]].
9. Click the button for the type of sale: [[Cash Sale]] (paid in full), [[Credit Sale]] (the customer pays later) or [[Save as Pending]] (kept for later) — see section 4.2.
10. If receipt printing is enabled, BizTrack shows the receipt (or prints it straight to the printer — see section 4.4).

Use [[Clear]] to remove everything from the sale and start again.

> NOTE: Scanning a barcode with the camera or a scanner adds the product even if it is not in the short list on screen.
> WARNING: If you try to sell more than the quantity available, BizTrack warns you — unless Allow Negative Stock is enabled in Settings. A credit sale needs a customer, and it is refused when the customer's credit limit would be exceeded (if the credit limit is switched on).

## 4.2  Sale Types

| Type | Description |
| Cash Sale | Full payment is received at once and the sale is completed. |
| Credit Sale | The customer pays later; the debt is recorded under Debits. A customer is required. |
| Pending Order | The sale is saved without being completed — for example the goods are not collected yet or payment is not complete — and can be finished later. A customer is required. |

## 4.3  Pending Orders

PATH: [[Sales]] › [[Pending Orders]]

Here you see all orders that were saved but not yet completed.

1. Go to [[Sales]] → [[Pending Orders]].
2. Search for an order by number or customer.
3. Use the row actions: [[View]] opens the order details; [[Edit]] reopens it on the Invoice page; [[Cancel order]] cancels it; [[Delete]] removes it.
4. In the order details you can [[Record Payment]] (see the [[Payments]] list), [[Edit]] the order, or click [[Complete Sale]] to finish it as a sale.

> NOTE: Cancelling an order that already has payments asks for a note — [[Reason for cancelling]]. An order with payments cannot be deleted, only cancelled. After a cancelled order you can use [[Reopen]] if it needs to be worked on again.

## 4.4  Sales History

PATH: [[Sales]] › [[Sales History]]

A list of every sale, with search by invoice number or customer and filters for the date and type.

1. Go to [[Sales]] → [[Sales History]].
2. Click [[View]] to see the details of an invoice.
3. Click [[Receipt]] to reprint the receipt, [[Delivery Note]] to download a delivery note as a PDF, or [[Sales Return]] to return goods from this sale.

> NOTE: Receipt printing has two modes, set in Settings → Configurations. With [[Receipt Preview]] on, the receipt page opens first and you print from it. With Receipt Preview off (and [[Enable Receipt Printing]] on), the receipt is sent straight to the printer through QZ Tray. QZ Tray must be installed and running on the computer connected to the printer. On the receipt page, the button [[Print with QZ Tray]] sends the receipt straight to the printer too, and [[Print]] opens the browser's print window.

## 4.5  Sales Returns

PATH: [[Sales]] › [[Sales Returns]]

Use this whenever a customer returns something — because of a defect, a change of mind or another reason.

1. Go to [[Sales]] → [[Sales Returns]] and click [[New Return]] (or use [[Sales Return]] on the sale in Sales History).
2. Look up the invoice by its number.
3. For each product being returned, enter the [[Return qty]] (it cannot be more than what was sold), the [[Condition]] of the goods and whether to [[Restock]] them.
4. Choose the [[Refund Method]] and give a [[Reason]], then click [[Save]]. The return is saved as pending.
5. A user with the right permission opens the return and clicks [[Approve]] (or [[Reject]]).

> NOTE: Stock for the returned goods is added back, and the refund or the customer's balance is updated, when the return is approved. Goods that are damaged should not be marked to restock.

## 4.6  Debits

PATH: [[Sales]] › [[Debits]]

Every customer with an outstanding balance from credit sales, with due dates and overdue status.

1. Go to [[Sales]] → [[Debits]].
2. Click [[View]] to see the invoice and its [[Payments]].
3. Click [[Record Payment]] when the customer pays (in full or in part). Enter the amount and the payment method and save.
4. To remind the customer, click [[Send Reminder]]. A ready-made [[Message]] opens (friendly, or an overdue notice if the due date has passed). Edit it if you wish and click [[Send]].
5. Click [[Mark Overdue]] at the top to flag every debt that has passed its due date.

> NOTE: The reminder goes out as an SMS through your business's SMS service — see Chapter 8.

## 4.7  Customers

PATH: [[Sales]] › [[Customers]]

1. Go to [[Sales]] → [[Customers]].
2. Click [[New Customer]].
3. Fill in the customer's name, phone number, email and address as your store requires.
4. If the customer may buy on credit, set the [[Credit Limit]] and [[Credit Due Days]].
5. Click [[Save]].

> NOTE: Use [[Walk-in Customer]] for cash sales where you do not need to register the customer's details. The customer list also shows each customer's [[Current Balance]].

@@CH 5
@@TITLE Purchases

The Purchases section covers the whole buying cycle with your suppliers: ordering, receiving goods, checking invoices and paying. It is under the [[Purchases]] menu.

## 5.1  Suppliers

PATH: [[Purchases]] › [[Suppliers]]

1. Go to [[Purchases]] → [[Suppliers]].
2. Click [[New Supplier]].
3. Fill in the [[Supplier Name]], [[Contact Person]], [[Phone]], [[Email]], address, [[City]] and [[Country]].
4. Enter the [[Payment Terms]] and the [[TIN Number]] / [[VAT Number]] if they apply.
5. Click [[Save]].

## 5.2  Purchase Orders

PATH: [[Purchases]] › [[Purchase Orders]] › [[New Purchase Order]]
PERM: view_purchase_orders / create_purchase_orders

A purchase order (PO) is a formal request to a supplier for goods, made before they are received. The page for a new order is a full page, like an invoice, so the supplier, items, costs and totals stay in view while you work.

1. Go to [[Purchases]] → [[Purchase Orders]] and click [[New Purchase Order]].
2. Select the [[Supplier]].
3. Add the products, with the [[Quantity]] and [[Unit Price]] (and [[Discount]] and [[Tax]] where used). On a phone each item is a card.
4. Enter the [[Expected delivery]] date, the [[Payment Terms]] and the [[Delivery address]] if needed, and any [[Notes]].
5. Click [[Save]]. The order is saved as pending approval and can still be edited until it is approved.
6. A user with the right permission opens the order and clicks [[Approve]] (or [[Reject]], giving a [[Reason]]).
7. When the goods arrive, click [[Receive Goods]] on the approved order (section 5.3).

> NOTE: An order moves through the statuses pending approval, approved, partially received, received or cancelled. The order list shows what is already [[Received]] and still [[Pending]] for each item.

## 5.3  Receiving Goods — GRN (Goods Received Note)

PATH: [[Purchases]] › [[Goods Received]]

The GRN records the goods that really arrived and adds them to your stock. If your store requires inspection, the stock is added only after the goods are inspected and approved.

1. Go to [[Purchases]] → [[Goods Received]] and click [[Receive goods]] (or click [[Receive Goods]] on the purchase order).
2. Select the [[Purchase Order]]. Its products are filled in.
3. For each product, enter the quantity [[Received]] (it may differ from what was ordered and cannot be more than is still [[Pending]]).
4. If you use batches, fill in the [[Batch number]], the [[Manufactured Date]] and the [[Expiry Date]]. Check the [[Unit cost]] and, where it is asked for, the [[Selling Price]].
5. Add the supplier's delivery note and invoice numbers if you have them, then click [[Save]].
6. If inspection is required, the GRN waits as pending inspection. A user with the right permission clicks [[Inspect]], enters how many units were [[Accepted]] and how many [[Rejected]] (with a [[Rejection Reason]]) and confirms. The accepted quantity is then added to stock. If inspection is not required, the stock is added as soon as you save.

> WARNING: Accepted plus rejected cannot be more than the quantity received, and the quantity received cannot be more than what is still pending on the purchase order.

## 5.4  Supplier Invoices

PATH: [[Purchases]] › [[Supplier Invoices]]

After receiving goods, record the supplier's invoice to show what you owe.

1. Go to [[Purchases]] → [[Supplier Invoices]] and click [[New invoice]].
2. Select the [[GRN]] to fill in the items and amounts.
3. Enter the supplier's invoice number ([[Supplier Inv. No.]]), the [[Invoice Date]] and the [[Due Date]], and any [[Shipping Cost]] or [[Other Charges]].
4. Click [[Save]]. A user with the right permission can [[Approve]] the invoice.
5. To pay it, click [[Record Payment]] (section 5.5).

## 5.5  Supplier Payments

PATH: [[Purchases]] › [[Supplier Payments]]

1. Go to [[Purchases]] → [[Supplier Payments]] and click [[New payment]] (or click [[Record Payment]] on a supplier invoice).
2. Select the supplier invoice you are paying.
3. Enter the amount and the payment method (for a bank payment also the bank name and account number), and a reference number.
4. Click [[Save]] — the invoice's [[Balance Due]] goes down.

> NOTE: A payment made by mistake can be cancelled with [[Cancel payment]]; the invoice balance is restored.

## 5.6  Supplier Returns

PATH: [[Purchases]] › [[Supplier Returns]]

1. Go to [[Purchases]] → [[Supplier Returns]] and click [[New Return]].
2. Select the [[GRN]] to return goods from.
3. For each product, enter the [[Return qty]] (it cannot be more than what is [[Returnable]]) and the [[Condition]].
4. Give the [[Return Reason]] and the [[Refund Method]], then click [[Save]].
5. A user with the right permission clicks [[Approve]] — the stock and the related supplier invoice are adjusted.

@@CH 7
@@TITLE Reports

The Reports section shows how your business is performing — sales, profit, purchases, inventory and expenses — for any period you choose.

> NOTE: Every report can be exported as PDF, Excel or CSV, or printed, from the export button above the report table. Reports are open to the roles that have the matching permission.

## 7.1  How Every Report Works

Every report page works the same way:

1. Click the name of the report you want in the row of report names at the top of the page.
2. Choose the period with [[Start Date]] and [[End Date]], or use a shortcut: [[Today]], [[Yesterday]], [[This Week]], [[This Month]], [[Last Month]] or [[This Year]]. Some reports also have filters such as category, product or sale type.
3. The summary cards above the table show the main totals.
4. A chart appears above the table for many reports: trend and comparison reports draw a line chart, and reports that rank things (by product, cashier, customer, category, payment method, supplier, fast moving, dead stock, profit reports) draw a bar chart of the ten biggest rows.
5. The table shows the columns of that report in a fixed order. Use the search box to find rows. A [[Total]] row under the table adds up the money and quantity columns, for all rows of the report and not only the page you see.
6. Use the export button to download a PDF, an Excel file or a CSV file, or to print. The same Total row, and the period, are included.

> NOTE: On a phone the table shows each row as a card and the summary cards show the totals. If you have the AI permission, the button above the report can explain the figures in plain words.

@@CH 15
@@TITLE General Tips & Troubleshooting

## 15.1  Tips for Getting the Most out of BizTrack

- Do a Stock Count regularly to catch shrinkage early.
- Set an accurate Alert Quantity for every product so you are warned before it runs out.
- Review Debits regularly and send payment reminders before the due date.
- Make sure you work in the correct store before you start selling or buying (for businesses with several stores).
- Download a backup regularly (Settings → Tools) and ask support about automatic backups.
- Give administrative permissions (Users, Settings, Security) only to trusted people.
- Use the Profit Reports and the Net Profit Report every month to see which products and costs matter most.
- Close each day with the Day Report.
- Save the messages you send often as Templates, and keep your customers in Groups so one click reaches everyone.
- On a phone, install BizTrack as an app (section 1.9) for faster opening.

## 15.2  Common Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
| I cannot see a menu item | Ask your store administrator — your role may not have permission for that section (Users → Permissions). |
| I forgot my password | Click Forgot password? on the login page (section 1.4). |
| Sign-in says to wait a few minutes | There were 10 wrong passwords for that email in 15 minutes. Wait the time shown, or use Forgot password? (section 1.2). |
| The screen says Session Locked | You were inactive for a while. Enter your password and click Unlock (section 1.8). |
| A page shows Something went wrong | Click Try again; if it comes back, click Reload. Anything already saved is safe. If it keeps happening on the same page, send a ticket saying which page it was (section 14.2). |
| A message says A new version is available | BizTrack was updated while the page was open. Click Reload. |
| The system shows old data | Go to Settings → Tools → Clear Cache, then reload the page. |
| I cannot sell a product — stock is empty | Receive the goods with a GRN, or add the quantity with a Stock Adjustment. |
| The price does not look right | Check the Price Categories and the Price List — the product may be using another price category. |
| A customer or product does not show when I search | Type part of the name or phone number — the search covers all records. If it still does not appear, check the spelling or whether the item is inactive. |
| The receipt does not print straight away | Check that Enable Receipt Printing is on and that QZ Tray is running on the computer connected to the printer. Use Print on the receipt page to print with the browser instead. |
| SMS says it is not set up yet | The SMS service for your business must be set up (Settings → Integration, or by the BizTrack team) — see Chapter 14. |
| I need more SMS credit | Open Messaging → Account & Top-up. Pay directly if you see payment cards, or send a top-up request (section 8.6). |
| I ran into an error I do not understand | Send a ticket describing exactly what happened (Chapter 14). |
